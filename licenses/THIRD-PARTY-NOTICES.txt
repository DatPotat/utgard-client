Utgard - third-party components
===============================

Parson 1.5.4
  Compiled into utgard.exe.
  License: MIT - full text in PARSON-MIT.txt.
  Source: https://github.com/kgabis/parson

sing-box 1.14.1
  NOT included in the Utgard archive. On first start Utgard offers to
  download the official release from
  https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box/releases/tag/v1.14.1
  and verifies it against SHA-256 checksums built into utgard.exe.
  sing-box runs as a separate program; it is not linked into utgard.exe.
  License: GPL-3.0-or-later. The LICENSE file from the official archive is
  unpacked next to sing-box.exe in the sing-box folder.
  Source: https://github.com/SagerNet/sing-box/tree/v1.14.1
  libcronet.dll arrives in the same official archive; Utgard does not
  redistribute it.
